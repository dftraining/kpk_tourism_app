Peshawar

A historic crossroads of cultures with vibrant bazaars and ancient sites.

Tourist Spots

  • Peshawar Gandhara Museum - Artifacts from Buddhist era.
  • Khyber Pass - Iconic mountain route.
  • Qissa Khawani Bazaar - Storytellers' market.
  • Bala Hisar Fort - Ancient hilltop fortress.
  • Sethi House Museum - Ornate 19th-century mansion.

Recommended Hotels

  • Pearl Continental Peshawar - Luxury city center.
  • Shelton House - Boutique heritage stay.
  • Crown Plaza Peshawar - Modern comfort.
